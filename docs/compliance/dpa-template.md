# CareOps Data Processing Addendum (Template)

## Parties
- Controller: Care provider organisation.
- Processor: EXA-TECH (CareOps).

## Subject Matter
Processing of adult social care operational exports for quality, safety, compliance, and management intelligence workflows.

## Duration
For the term of pilot and any agreed extension.

## Nature and Purpose
- Parse and structure exported records.
- Surface risk signals and operational gaps.
- Generate evidence-linked manager outputs.

## Categories of Data Subjects
- Service users.
- Care staff.
- Operational managers.

## Categories of Personal Data
- Identifiers, care notes, incident records, rota context, staff records.

## Processor Obligations
- Process only on documented instruction.
- Maintain confidentiality and access controls.
- Support controller rights and incident response.
- Assist with audits and compliance evidence.

## Security Controls (Pilot)
- Authenticated access gate.
- Session controls and sign-out.
- Local-first data handling model.
- Audit trail for critical report generation paths.

## Sub-processors
No care-content sub-processing by default workflow. Any future sub-processor use requires controller notice and agreement.

## Deletion/Return
At pilot end, controller instructs deletion/retention actions. Local browser purge procedures and exported artefact handling are controller-run with processor guidance.
